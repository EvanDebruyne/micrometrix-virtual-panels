      About this demo

      These panels are browser simulations built from the instruments' own firmware source, not videos or mock-ups. Screens, menus, the keypad, the controller, the clean cycle, the titration logic, Signal Health and the file formats follow the firmware. The sensor, the sample, the water and the pump are simulated, and the history shown when the page opens is generated so the graphs are not empty.
